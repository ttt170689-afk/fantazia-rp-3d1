# Update 51 — mourners around the coffin

## Visible changes

The normal funeral path no longer puts the same Lee Perry-Smith scanned head on
five generated bodies. It loads five separate, fully dressed MakeHuman-derived
GLBs: two older people, two younger men, and a younger woman. Each has different
anatomy/face geometry and skin; the cast uses four hair shapes, different heights,
dark suits/blouses/skirts and shoes. This is a substantial replacement, not a
claim of literal “100×” quality or photorealism.

The models include articulated fingers, fitted sleeves, collar/lapels, textured
skin, eyes with visible irises and alpha-tested hair. Occluded torso/hip skin is
removed from the export to avoid showing through garments.

Acting is authored on the skeleton: breathing, bowed heads, hands together,
a hand raised to the face, and the existing head-before-shoulders turn. Two-bone
arm reach keeps wrists at their intended locations. Hips, legs and shoes remain
planted. Reduced-motion mode suppresses idle motion but preserves the narrative
turn. Animation is deterministic and remains driven by the native story loop.

The coffin has dark wood grain, non-emissive brass handles and petalled flowers.
The ground has a small deterministic surface texture, with contact shadows under
the people and coffin that also work when shadow maps are disabled. The funeral
pass uses sRGB output for its GLTF textures, so dark suits and skin do not collapse
into black under the legacy renderer. Output encoding is restored after the pass,
including renderer failures; the other scenes retain their existing rendering.

## Runtime and failure behavior

- Five self-contained local GLBs; 13,445,504 bytes total, no CDN texture fetches.
- 177,614 triangles and 25 mesh draws for the entire cast; 512px embedded maps.
- One cached asset per profile, one independent skeleton per actor. Five meshes
  share the actor's skeleton, not another actor's bones. Scene teardown disposes
  bone textures; immutable geometry/materials/maps remain in the page cache.
- Single-flight preload; 18-second per-file deadline with abortable fetch. After
  Play, preload finishes before CPU-heavy city construction can starve image
  decode callbacks. Seen stories bypass this preload; the logo/menu does not load it.
- HTTP failures, corrupt GLBs or deadlines use the old **clothed geometry-only
  fallback** for the failed person only. Diagnostics report this; fallback is not
  passed off as the new high-detail cast. No mid-scene replacement if parse finishes
  late; late resources are disposed. Reload can retry after a network failure.
- The game avatar, voices, story timing, saves, public guest access, bank/home
  interiors, stairs and lifts are unchanged. No production-account operations.

## Source/license

The core mesh/morph/rig/eyes are CC0. Older clothing/hair and converted textures
are distributed under their AGPLv3 notices; generated character assets use
AGPL-3.0-or-later. No unsupported assumption of blanket CC0 licensing.

`public/assets/licenses/funeral51-source.md` has pinned upstream revisions,
credits, license texts and rebuild instructions. A source archive includes all
47 input files plus the original conversion script. SHA-256 input digests and an
archive-consistency test guard against missing/edit-stale corresponding source.
No MakeHuman application code or gameplay avatar is imported.
